Why Yangon

A table built on sharing

There's a dish in Burma called lahpet — fermented tea leaves, tossed with crunch and heat and a little sourness, meant to be passed from one plate at the center of the table. That idea, more than any single recipe, is what Yangon is built around: food that brings people together, served the way it would be at home, just with a little more polish on the table settings.

We cook Burmese dishes you may never have tried, alongside familiar Mandarin favorites, because that's honestly how our kitchen grew up eating — and we think Burlingame is ready to fall in love with both.
